## Overview

### Who produces it?

China dominates primary supply. Baowu is a leading integrated group; Fugu’s many producers form a major cluster. Outside China, established supply includes RIMA, ICL Dead Sea Magnesium, Solikamsk, UKTMP and Kar Mineral. The directory separates current commercial evidence, interruptions, older evidence and downstream processors. [S01](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-magnesium-metal.pdf) [S02](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225223859.PDF) [S53](https://www.cmgroup.net/news/fugu-aims-more-than-100-kt-of-mg-alloy-production-in-2026/)

### What do they start with?

Most covered Chinese operations, RIMA and Kar Mineral use dolomite. ICL uses Dead Sea brine-derived carnallite; chloride-fed plants also include Solikamsk and UKTMP. Recyclers start with metal scrap and purchased metal. Exact quarry contracts and numerical feed assays are often undisclosed. [S02](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225223859.PDF) [S18](https://financialfilings.com/filings/icl-group-ltd/annual-report/2026/32926978/) [S23](https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=501871) [S28](https://www.uralkali.com/upload/iblock/d1f/ybkimixkd5jmjdoarqtsrtnums0m1hai/uralkali_ar2024_eng.pdf) [S33](https://bizup.kz/ru/documents/p2500000339) [S35](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/TUR_NID_2026.pdf) [S45](https://magontec.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Magontec-Limited-2025-Annual-Report-Final.pdf)

### How is the metal made?

Thermal producers heat dolomite into a reactive oxide mixture, then use a silicon-bearing material to release magnesium. Electrolytic producers prepare dry magnesium chloride and use electricity to separate the metal. Both routes finish with refining and casting or alloying. Recycling starts with existing metal and remelts it. [S13](https://wap.asianmetal.com/cag/2025/visit2026020402En.shtml) [S21](https://www.confer.cz/metal/2016/download/1804-aluminium-and-magnesium-production-changes-and-perspectives.pdf) [S23](https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=501871) [S33](https://bizup.kz/ru/documents/p2500000339) [S45](https://magontec.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Magontec-Limited-2025-Annual-Report-Final.pdf)

### How much?

USGS estimates 1.1 million t of world primary production in 2025, including 950,000 t in China. ICL reports about 17,800 t of metal in 2025. Many other company figures are nameplates, old actuals, sales or mixed-material totals; a reliable current company league table cannot be constructed from the reviewed public evidence. [S01](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-magnesium-metal.pdf) [S18](https://financialfilings.com/filings/icl-group-ltd/annual-report/2026/32926978/)

### Who buys it?

Evidence usually identifies sectors rather than direct ingot buyers. Documented links include Baowu component deliveries to Geely StarDrive, VREMT and ZF, UKTMP’s internal titanium production, and Magontec’s automotive scrap loop. Future project offtake and letters of intent are kept separate. [S02](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225223859.PDF) [S32](https://www.uktmp.kz/en/production) [S45](https://magontec.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Magontec-Limited-2025-Annual-Report-Final.pdf) [S56](https://financialfilings.com/filings/latrobe-magnesium-limited/annual-report/2026/61546901/)

## How magnesium metal is produced

Follow the material through preparation, extraction, refining and saleable products. The guide explains the two main primary routes and the separate recycling route, then shows what is documented at individual facilities.

### Three routes, three starting points

The key question is whether the incoming magnesium is chemically bound in a mineral or salt, or already present as metal. Primary extraction breaks that chemical bond. Recycling starts with existing metal. Subsequent alloying and casting can occur at the smelter or at another company; they do not create additional primary magnesium. [S107](https://www.intlmag.org/page/basics_about_mg_ima) [S113](https://www.intlmag.org/page/sustain_recycle_ima) [S114](https://www.intlmag.org/page/resource_process_ima)

**Dolomite → primary metal:** Crush → calcine into oxides → mix with ferrosilicon and compact → heat under vacuum → condense magnesium vapor → refine and cast. [S112](https://scrreen.eu/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/SCRREEN2_factsheets_MAGNESIUM.pdf)

**Brine or carnallite → primary metal:** Concentrate and purify → prepare dry chloride → electrolyze molten salt → separate magnesium and chlorine → refine and cast or use internally. [S107](https://www.intlmag.org/page/basics_about_mg_ima)

**Metal scrap → secondary alloy:** Sort by alloy and contamination → prepare scrap → remelt and clean → adjust composition → cast qualified alloy for reuse. [S108](https://www.epa.gov/sites/production/files/2020-11/documents/c12s12.pdf) [S113](https://www.intlmag.org/page/sustain_recycle_ima)

### 1. Dolomite and silicothermic reduction

Silicothermic means that silicon removes oxygen from a magnesium oxide feed. Ferrosilicon supplies the silicon; it is a consumed reagent, not simply furnace fuel. Pidgeon is the familiar version of this route, but furnace arrangements and operating cycles differ. RIMA describes its own process, and Baowu reports vertical-retort technology. [S02](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225223859.PDF) [S23](https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=501871) [S107](https://www.intlmag.org/page/basics_about_mg_ima)

1. **Prepare and calcine the dolomite.** Dolomite contains magnesium and calcium carbonates. Crushing and heating remove carbonate as carbon dioxide, leaving a reactive mixture of magnesium oxide and calcium oxide, called dolime. SCRREEN2 describes calcination at roughly 1,000–1,300 °C; this is a route-level range, not a set point assigned to every producer. No magnesium metal has been made at this stage. [S112](https://scrreen.eu/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/SCRREEN2_factsheets_MAGNESIUM.pdf)

2. **Make the reduction charge.** The calcined material is ground, mixed with the silicon-bearing reductant and compacted into briquettes. Intimate contact between the solids helps the reaction proceed. Calcium fluoride is used as an additive in conventional Pidgeon descriptions, but its use and dosage should not be assumed at every plant. The prepared charge is therefore different from both the mined rock and the calcined oxide. [S107](https://www.intlmag.org/page/basics_about_mg_ima) [S112](https://scrreen.eu/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/SCRREEN2_factsheets_MAGNESIUM.pdf)

3. **Release magnesium under vacuum.** Heating the charge makes magnesium vapor. Reduced pressure helps remove that vapor from the reacting solids and favors continued production. Calcium oxide helps bind the silicon and oxygen in a calcium-silicate residue. A simplified net reaction is: 2MgO + 2CaO + Si → 2Mg(vapor) + Ca2SiO4. This shows silicon as the active reagent; commercial ferrosilicon also brings iron into the charge. [S111](https://publications.anl.gov/anlpubs/2014/09/107574.pdf)

4. **Condense the vapor.** The vapor reaches a cooler collection zone and deposits as solid magnesium, often called a crown. This vapor separation leaves much of the nonvolatile material behind. The crown then needs melting and finishing before sale as standard ingot or alloy. Residue remains in the reduction vessel and must be removed between batches in conventional batch operation. [S107](https://www.intlmag.org/page/basics_about_mg_ima) [S112](https://scrreen.eu/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/SCRREEN2_factsheets_MAGNESIUM.pdf)

A useful experimental illustration: a 2005 Turkish study used calcined dolomite assaying 43.20% MgO and 47.46% CaO, with ferrosilicon containing 75% Si and 24% Fe, at 1,200 °C and 1 mbar (100 Pa). It varied time, reductant and additives. These are stated oxide/feed assays and laboratory conditions—not the composition of raw ore, a Kar Mineral plant specification, or a commercial recovery guarantee. [S110](https://www.scientific.net/MSF.488-489.39)

### 2. Chloride preparation and electrolysis

Electrolytic production uses a molten salt containing magnesium chloride. The brine from a lake or the carnallite from a mine is several preparation steps away from that cell feed. The starting source determines the purification and dehydration sequence. [S107](https://www.intlmag.org/page/basics_about_mg_ima) [S33](https://bizup.kz/ru/documents/p2500000339)

1. **Concentrate and separate the salts.** Evaporation and crystallization can recover magnesium-bearing salts from brine. Carnallite also contains potassium chloride and water bound in its crystals. Separation and purification manage unwanted salts and insoluble material before final dehydration. At Dead Sea Magnesium, the documented chain starts with Dead Sea brine-derived carnallite within the integrated ICL system. [S18](https://financialfilings.com/filings/icl-group-ltd/annual-report/2026/32926978/) [S20](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/news/press_16092024/en/PressReleases_eng_press_16092024_file2.pdf)

2. **Remove bound water without losing chloride.** Heating hydrated MgCl2 can cause hydrolysis: water reacts with the chloride, producing magnesium hydroxychloride and eventually MgO while releasing HCl. This is why preparing dry chloride requires controlled chemistry, rather than only evaporating free water. A 2023 diffraction study directly observed the sequence. These laboratory transformations explain the challenge; their temperatures are not prescribed plant settings. [S109](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022459623001330)

3. **Condition the prepared salt.** Industrial schemes can combine staged dehydration, melting, chlorination of oxide impurities and settling. The Kazakhstan reference describes this sequence for carnallite preparation. Its prepared-feed limits include MgCl2 ≥50% for grade A or ≥48% for grade B, with MgO ≤0.80% and carbon ≤0.08%. These percentages describe a salt mixture, not metal purity. [S33](https://bizup.kz/ru/documents/p2500000339)

4. **Use electricity to separate metal and chlorine.** Direct current produces magnesium at the cathode and chlorine at the anode: MgCl2 → Mg + Cl2. Other chlorides help give the bath suitable melting and flow properties. IMA describes electrolysis at 655–720 °C, an indicative industry range. Magnesium is collected as molten metal; keeping it apart from chlorine prevents the products from reacting back together. [S107](https://www.intlmag.org/page/basics_about_mg_ima) [S33](https://bizup.kz/ru/documents/p2500000339)

5. **Withdraw and finish the metal.** The collected magnesium is separated from entrained salt, refined and cast, or transferred as liquid metal to an integrated operation. Chlorine has its own recovery and consumption route. At a titanium complex, MgCl2 generated when magnesium reduces titanium chloride can return to electrolysis; the same magnesium can therefore circulate repeatedly. [S32](https://www.uktmp.kz/en/production) [S33](https://bizup.kz/ru/documents/p2500000339)

Electrolyte composition, dry-feed composition and saleable metal purity are three different analyses. A high magnesium-chloride recovery in a preparation trial is also different from recovery of magnesium metal across the whole plant. Qinghai’s 2025 trial disclosures illustrate this distinction. [S80](https://www.cnmn.com.cn/ShowNews1.aspx?id=461529)

### 3. From crude metal to a saleable product

Finishing has two jobs: remove unwanted material and deliver the required composition. Solid thermal crowns must first be melted; electrolytic metal arrives molten but can carry salt. In flux-based treatment, a salt mixture helps collect nonmetallic material and separate it from the melt. Settling and skimming separate cleaner metal from residues. The EPA description is a historical example, not a universal modern operating recipe. [S108](https://www.epa.gov/sites/production/files/2020-11/documents/c12s12.pdf)

The melt also needs protection from oxidation. Flux covers and protective gas systems are used, with the choice depending on the process and facility. Older technical references discuss gases that should not be assumed to describe a producer’s present practice. A current plant assessment needs its actual cover system and emissions data. [S111](https://publications.anl.gov/anlpubs/2014/09/107574.pdf)

For alloy ingot, additions set the intended composition. For unalloyed ingot, impurity limits remain the target. Sampling, chemical acceptance and identification of the melt connect production to the buyer’s specification. UKTMP’s technical reference describes sampling and marking ingots with grade and melt identification. [S33](https://bizup.kz/ru/documents/p2500000339) [S95](https://store.astm.org/b0092_b0092m-17r25.html)

Die casting, billet extrusion, rolling, machining and powder manufacture are later product routes. These steps change shape or properties; their tonnage must not be added to the primary metal from which they started. General fabrication routes do not prove that a particular smelter supplies a particular OEM. [S114](https://www.intlmag.org/page/resource_process_ima)

### 4. Recycling and the limits of remelting

1. **Keep compatible material together.** Clean diecasting returns of a known alloy are easier to return to the same specification than mixed end-of-life scrap. Sorting matters because coatings, fasteners and other metals can introduce iron, nickel or copper. Machining chips require attention to lubricants, moisture and oxidation; Luxfer’s guidance stresses the condition and segregation of this stream. [S113](https://www.intlmag.org/page/sustain_recycle_ima) [S51](https://www.luxfermeltechnologies.com/knowledge-hub/machined-magnesium-guide/)

2. **Remelt and separate unwanted material.** Recycling can use flux refining, or fluxless melting followed by settling or gas treatment. The objective is to recover usable metal while controlling inclusions and composition. Oxides and processing residues are separate from recovered alloy, so scrap charged is not the same quantity as saleable metal. [S108](https://www.epa.gov/sites/production/files/2020-11/documents/c12s12.pdf) [S113](https://www.intlmag.org/page/sustain_recycle_ima)

3. **Correct the chemistry and recast.** Remelting alone does not remove every dissolved impurity. IMA describes manganese treatment for iron control, while nickel and copper may require dilution or distillation. The chosen treatment depends on the alloy and contamination; it is not automatically part of every recycler’s process. Qualified recycled alloy can re-enter a casting loop. [S113](https://www.intlmag.org/page/sustain_recycle_ima)

Magontec and AMACOR belong in this metal-scrap/alloy chain. Their recycled output must remain separate from new primary metal. Magnesium retained within recycled aluminum alloy is another boundary: recycling an aluminum can does not ordinarily yield a separate magnesium ingot. [S45](https://magontec.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Magontec-Limited-2025-Annual-Report-Final.pdf) [S48](https://amacor.us/products) [S111](https://publications.anl.gov/anlpubs/2014/09/107574.pdf)

### Reading feed and energy numbers correctly

A feed-to-product ratio needs both ends named. Mined dolomite, calcined dolime and briquetted charge have different masses. Likewise, a tonne of hydrated carnallite is not a tonne of anhydrous MgCl2. Some reported ratios describe a mixture recipe; others describe a recovery percentage or a plant-wide material inventory. They cannot be substituted for one another.

The historical example below gives an order of magnitude for a published process inventory. It is not a procurement forecast or a claim about current operating plants. Energy comparisons must include the same stages. A cell-only electricity number omits chloride preparation and finishing. ### Why the process changes the supply picture

For thermal plants, the feed system includes both the magnesium-bearing mineral and the reductant. A captive dolomite mine alone does not remove dependence on ferrosilicon, silicon or energy. At Yide, integrated ferrosilicon is specifically documented; elsewhere the purchasing boundary can remain undisclosed. [S13](https://wap.asianmetal.com/cag/2025/visit2026020402En.shtml)

For chloride plants, the preparation section must deliver a salt that the cells can use consistently. Qinghai’s reports of impurity control and trial metal production show why a design nameplate cannot stand in for proven, sustained output. At a titanium complex, internal chloride recycling makes gross cell output especially easy to misread as fresh supply. [S80](https://www.cnmn.com.cn/ShowNews1.aspx?id=461529) [S32](https://www.uktmp.kz/en/production)

Alternative feedstocks and proposed processes remain in the project directory. For example, Verde’s disclosed aluminothermic trial uses aluminum as reductant, while other projects emphasize residue upgrading or chloride production. A successful concentrate, MgO or chloride stage is progress toward metal production; it is not itself magnesium-metal output. [S75](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.intlmag.org/resource/collection/1B278489-BEAC-4779-8813-E1E6F7D06DEA/IMA-Verde-Magnesium-Alex-Rosu-Tues-11-20-11-40.pdf) [S56](https://financialfilings.com/filings/latrobe-magnesium-limited/annual-report/2026/61546901/)

Public evidence still does not establish current plant-wide recovery, energy consumption, reagent consumption or impurity-removal performance for most producers. Those values remain undisclosed here. The deeper process description explains the disclosed route without turning a generic flowsheet into an audited plant balance.

## Magnesium hydroxide as a feedstock

Mg(OH)₂ can connect brine recovery or mineral supply to magnesium-metal production. Compare its origins, preparation routes, commercial grades and usable magnesium content before choosing a feed.

### Where hydroxide fits

Research supplement: 28 September 2026. Magnesium hydroxide is a magnesium compound, not metal. It can be purchased as powder or aqueous slurry, recovered as a precipitate from brine, or obtained from the natural mineral brucite. The useful comparison is the cost and quality of magnesium delivered to the chosen extraction process. Compound production is kept separate from the atlas’s primary-metal totals. [S122](https://magnesiaspecialties.com/company/process) [S125](https://www.lehvoss.de/en/Magnesia/products/magnesium-hydroxide) [S127](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/magnesium-compounds-statistics-and-information)

Hydroxide can provide a solid intermediate that is washed, sampled and transported before metal extraction. Its value depends on what happens next: dissolution into chloride, or calcination into MgO followed by a compatible metal-making process. A hydroxide assay does not establish the purity or yield of the eventual ingot. [S121](https://archive.epa.gov/epawaste/nonhaz/industrial/special/web/pdf/part6.pdf) [S122](https://magnesiaspecialties.com/company/process)

**Recover the magnesium:** Brine + alkaline reagent → precipitate → settle, filter and wash → Mg(OH)₂.

**Prepare chloride:** Mg(OH)₂ + HCl → MgCl₂ solution → purification and dehydration → chloride electrolysis.

**Prepare oxide:** Mg(OH)₂ + heat → MgO + water vapor → compatible oxide-fed extraction process.

### Origins and preparation

Synthetic hydroxide from brine is already a commercial compound route. Martin Marietta describes reacting natural brine with dolomitic lime and water, then settling, filtering and washing the solids. Its Manistee, Michigan operation is a documented domestic example. This identifies a potential feedstock supply chain; it does not establish spare capacity or a contract to supply a metal producer. [S122](https://magnesiaspecialties.com/company/process) [S128](https://magnesiaspecialties.com/blogs/benefits-of-flomag-magnesium-hydroxide-slurry-for-wastewater-treatment)

Natural brucite takes a mineral route. LEHVOSS lists granular and milled natural grades alongside synthetic products. A named grade’s assay should guide screening: geological availability alone says little about the delivered impurity load. Hydroxide made by hydrating purchased MgO is another route described in the technical literature; for an oxide-fed plant, buying that hydroxide and calcining it back to MgO adds a conversion loop that needs a specific benefit. The latter is an engineering inference. [S125](https://www.lehvoss.de/en/Magnesia/products/magnesium-hydroxide) [S126](https://iris.unipa.it/retrieve/5a7a0590-5b28-409f-8cd2-4ed1512bd3b1/acs.iecr.2c02935.pdf)

For new brine recovery, precipitation is only the first step. In 2022 pilot experiments, Morgante and colleagues found that recycling product as seed improved settling and filterability. Their feeds were synthetic MgCl₂-only solutions. The result supports attention to particle handling; it does not prove impurity removal from actual seawater or desalination concentrate. Test the real brine, reagent and wash circuit together. [S126](https://iris.unipa.it/retrieve/5a7a0590-5b28-409f-8cd2-4ed1512bd3b1/acs.iecr.2c02935.pdf)

### Two routes toward metal

1. **Convert to chloride.** The simplified reaction is Mg(OH)₂ + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + 2H₂O. EPA’s historical Dow account describes washed hydroxide, acid treatment, removal of calcium-bearing solids, further sulfate/boron purification, drying and electrolysis. The conversion creates water as well as chloride; downstream preparation remains necessary. [S121](https://archive.epa.gov/epawaste/nonhaz/industrial/special/web/pdf/part6.pdf)

2. **Match the chloride to the cell.** Most conventional chloride routes require tightly controlled dry feed. Historical Dow cells instead accepted partially hydrated chloride, with associated wet chlorine handling. That exception does not mean a wet hydroxide slurry can be fed directly to a conventional dry-chloride cell. Acid demand also needs a recycle boundary: Dow returned cell chlorine through an HCl-production step. [S121](https://archive.epa.gov/epawaste/nonhaz/industrial/special/web/pdf/part6.pdf) [S107](https://www.intlmag.org/page/basics_about_mg_ima)

3. **Or calcine to MgO.** Mg(OH)₂ → MgO + H₂O. Removing free moisture is drying; removing chemically bound water is dehydroxylation. Neither step makes metal. An MgO-fed extraction process must then remove oxygen. The atlas’s INFINIUM example is historical oxide-electrolysis research, not proof that a proposed hydroxide-to-metal plant is commercially qualified. [S122](https://magnesiaspecialties.com/company/process) [S116](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1431302)

4. **Validate the prepared feed.** Engineering implication: measure residual moisture, chemical impurities, particle size and process reactivity after preparation. Hydroxide-derived MgO also lacks the calcium-oxide component of calcined dolomite; it cannot be substituted by equal mass into a conventional Pidgeon charge without reformulating and testing that charge. [S111](https://publications.anl.gov/anlpubs/2014/09/107574.pdf)

The hydroxide-to-oxide reaction releases water rather than carbonate CO₂. That chemical fact is not a zero-emissions claim: upstream lime or MgO production, reagent manufacture, drying, calcination and metal extraction still belong in the assessment. [S122](https://magnesiaspecialties.com/company/process) [S111](https://publications.anl.gov/anlpubs/2014/09/107574.pdf)

### What makes it a useful U.S. feedstock?

The domestic brine-to-hydroxide chain makes purchased Mg(OH)₂ a credible candidate for development trials. The decision between that intermediate, direct chloride preparation and purchased MgO remains site-specific. The following screening steps are engineering recommendations, not published acceptance standards or a finding that one route is cheapest. [S128](https://magnesiaspecialties.com/blogs/benefits-of-flomag-magnesium-hydroxide-slurry-for-wastewater-treatment) [S122](https://magnesiaspecialties.com/company/process)

1. **Specify the feed and product together.** Request dry/wet basis, Mg-bearing phases, calcium, iron, silica, aluminum, chloride, sulfate, boron and any organic additives or coatings. Add trace-metal limits required by the intended metal buyer. An impurity can affect preparation, the cell or final metal; do not assume it all transfers to ingot. [S124](https://www.magnesiaspecialties.com/technical-data-sheets/MagChem_MH_10_ULC.pdf) [S121](https://archive.epa.gov/epawaste/nonhaz/industrial/special/web/pdf/part6.pdf)

2. **Run representative material through the entire route.** Measure filtration and wash losses, acid consumption or calcination behavior, prepared-feed chemistry, extraction recovery and accepted metal. Track impurities into reject streams as well as product. Synthetic-feed precipitation evidence cannot substitute for these trials. [S126](https://iris.unipa.it/retrieve/5a7a0590-5b28-409f-8cd2-4ed1512bd3b1/acs.iecr.2c02935.pdf)

3. **Compare delivered cost on recovered Mg.** Multiply a delivered feed quote in $/t by the calculated t feed/t Mg. Add preparation, heat, electricity, consumables and waste treatment; credit only defensible coproduct or reagent recovery. A slurry’s water adds freight and water handling; powder moves some processing upstream. No verified bulk hydroxide quote or complete hydroxide-to-metal cost was established in this review.

4. **Confirm supply at the intended scale.** Obtain plant origin, available annual dry tonnage, lot variability and transport terms. A product listing establishes an offering, not uncommitted capacity. Confirm that an otherwise attractive powder has no coating or additive incompatible with the process.

## Purity requirements and commodity flows

How product specifications shape who can buy magnesium, how the metal changes hands, and what regional price quotations actually mean.

### Purity is only one part of quality

A buyer purchases a defined material, not simply “magnesium.” Unalloyed ingot, a casting alloy, a powder and a sacrificial anode have different acceptance criteria. ASTM B92/B92M-17(2025) covers unalloyed ingot and stick for remelting; ISO 8287:2021 covers cast unalloyed magnesium made by thermal or electrolytic routes. Neither process name alone establishes the finished grade. [S95](https://store.astm.org/b0092_b0092m-17r25.html) [S96](https://www.iso.org/standard/75101.html)

Bulk assay tells how much magnesium is present. Individual impurity limits control what the remainder may contain. As a mass-fraction illustration, 99.9% Mg leaves at most 0.1%, or 1,000 ppm, for everything else combined; it does not allow 1,000 ppm of every impurity. A separate 0.001% limit corresponds to 10 ppm. These conversions explain the units, not a universal acceptance threshold.

A high-quality magnesium alloy intentionally contains other metals, so a lower Mg percentage need not mean lower quality. Nor does a high metal assay establish freedom from oxide inclusions, surface contamination or unsuitable particle size. Iron-related corrosion is documented experimentally; its effect depends on the material and environment, so no single corrosion-tolerance number is assigned to all products. [S98](https://magretech.us/products/az91d/) [S95](https://store.astm.org/b0092_b0092m-17r25.html) [S97](https://luxfermagtech.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Luxfer-Data-Sheets-Pure-Magnesium-Powders-for-Commercial-Applications-7.pdf) [S99](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26660298/)

FOB (free on board) places delivery on board the vessel at the named shipment port. For unit conversion, 1 US cent/lb is approximately USD 22.0462 per metric tonne. That arithmetic removes a unit difference only; it does not reconcile 99.8% ingot with 93%-basis alloy or remove regional trade barriers. A customs value divided by shipment mass is an average unit value, not automatically a like-for-like spot price.

## A U.S. case for electrochemical magnesium

The evidence supports developing dependable domestic primary supply. Electrochemistry offers a credible route if real feedstocks can be converted into qualified metal at a competitive total cost.

### The development case

The United States has an existing industrial need for magnesium but no primary production in the USGS 2025 series. Global primary supply is concentrated, while domestic recycling serves different material loops. Taken together, these facts motivate a U.S. process that converts locally available magnesium compounds into reliable, saleable metal. They establish a supply problem; they do not, by themselves, establish which new process will be cheapest. [S01](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-magnesium-metal.pdf)

This section is an engineering synthesis of the producer, feedstock, process and market evidence in this report, as of 27 September 2026. Its development recommendations are analysis, not a company feasibility study or an assertion that a proposed plant is operating. Electrolysis is already an industrial route: Dead Sea Magnesium operates chloride electrolysis, and US Magnesium used it historically. The opportunity is to improve the complete production chain for U.S. conditions. [S18](https://financialfilings.com/filings/icl-group-ltd/annual-report/2026/32926978/) [S21](https://www.confer.cz/metal/2016/download/1804-aluminium-and-magnesium-production-changes-and-perspectives.pdf) [S01](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-magnesium-metal.pdf)

**Supply need:** Concentrated world primary production + absent U.S. primary output → a reason to develop another dependable source.

**Technical opportunity:** Domestic brines or prepared oxide → controlled electrochemical conversion → specified metal or alloy feed.

**Commercial test:** Repeatable quality + sustained operation + competitive delivered cost → buyer qualification and contracted sales.

### Why investigate an electrochemical route?

Electrolysis uses electrical current to convert a prepared magnesium compound into metal. It can therefore shift the reduction step away from purchasing a silicon or aluminum reductant. It still needs heat, chemical preparation, suitable cell materials, metal recovery and product finishing. Cheap ore or brine does not automatically mean cheap cell feed. [S111](https://publications.anl.gov/anlpubs/2014/09/107574.pdf) [S116](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1431302)

For a chloride route, the commercial precedent is strong. The challenge is to purify and prepare a consistent chloride feed, manage moisture and impurities, separate metal from chlorine, and keep the system operating reliably. For an oxide-fed route, avoiding the chloride-preparation chain is a potential advantage, but it transfers the burden to a different electrolyte, durable anode system and metal-collection process. These are distinct development choices. [S21](https://www.confer.cz/metal/2016/download/1804-aluminium-and-magnesium-production-changes-and-perspectives.pdf) [S109](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022459623001330) [S116](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1431302)

| Route / precedent | Feed entering extraction | U.S. development opportunity — analysis | What remains to prove |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chloride electrolysis; operating overseas and historical U.S. precedent | Purified, suitably dehydrated chloride; not untreated brine | Adapt established metal extraction to a competitive domestic feed-preparation and power system. | Integrated feed cost, chlorine use or disposition, sustained recovery and availability. Magrathea’s pilot evidence is separate from commercial supply. [S21](https://www.confer.cz/metal/2016/download/1804-aluminium-and-magnesium-production-changes-and-perspectives.pdf) [S01](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-magnesium-metal.pdf) [S58](https://www.magratheametals.com/news/arkamagastm) |
| Oxide-fed electrolysis; historical INFINIUM research | Prepared MgO in a molten fluoride electrolyte in the documented research | Investigate a route that bypasses making a dry chloride feed. | Electrolyte management, oxygen-producing anode durability and magnesium collection at scale. The project later emphasized Mg–Nd master alloy, a different product. [S116](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1431302) |
| Aluminothermic reduction; Big Blue development | DOE describes dolomite plus aluminum scrap; purchased MgO is separately proposed | Benchmark another domestic route against the electrochemical options. | Continuous-run demonstration, final feed mix, product sales and commercial cost. [S117](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/mining/project-selections-foa-3105-critical-material-innovation-efficiency-and-alternatives) [S120](https://bigbluetech.com/big-blue-technologies-and-baymag-ink-supply-agreement/) |

The 2018 INFINIUM final report is useful evidence of both possibility and difficulty: its pure-magnesium work used oxygen-producing zirconia tubes and reported a tube campaign of up to 170 hours. That is research performance, not established commercial service life. Its later focus on a higher-value Mg–Nd master alloy also means that successful alloy production cannot be assumed to demonstrate commodity-ingot economics. [S116](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1431302)

The development hypothesis is that a well-integrated electrochemical plant can combine accessible U.S. feed with reliable power and qualified output. Low emissions are conditional: electricity generation, feed preparation, reagents, direct heat and waste treatment all belong in the comparison. An electrically driven cell alone does not establish a low-carbon or zero-emissions product. [S111](https://publications.anl.gov/anlpubs/2014/09/107574.pdf)

### Match a domestic feed to a real buyer

Arkansas provides a concrete chloride-feed example. Magrathea reports magnesium above 99.9% purity from TETRA Smackover brine, and the Arkansas Magnesium joint venture was established in March 2026. Its separate San Francisco Bay pilot has a Cargill sea-salt coproduct feed arrangement. These are two disclosed supply relationships, not interchangeable sources or proof of sustained annual production. Raw-brine assays, prepared-feed impurity limits and plant-wide feed consumption remain undisclosed in the reviewed material. [S58](https://www.magratheametals.com/news/arkamagastm) [S71](https://www.magratheametals.com/news/100million) [S72](https://www.magratheametals.com/news/cargill)

A domestic oxide route likewise needs an identified supply chain. The INFINIUM study used MgO as prepared feed; it does not establish a current mine-to-plant contract. Big Blue’s Baymag arrangement illustrates another boundary: metal made in the United States can still use an imported Canadian intermediate. Domestic extraction, North American sourcing and domestic metal conversion are separate claims. [S116](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1431302) [S120](https://bigbluetech.com/big-blue-technologies-and-baymag-ink-supply-agreement/)

The initial customer case can rest on existing uses. USGS estimates that 2025 reported U.S. primary consumption went to castings, principally automotive (69%), aluminum alloying (15%), iron and steel desulfurization (9%), and other uses (7%). These shares apply to that reported primary-consumption series, not to all recycled magnesium or all U.S. magnesium-containing products. They do not require a speculative magnesium-battery market to justify investigation. [S01](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-magnesium-metal.pdf)

A casting-alloy buyer needs consistent alloy chemistry and impurity control; an aluminum producer needs a reliable alloying addition; a steel-treatment customer may need a specified granule, powder or blend. A purity headline alone does not qualify metal for all three. Product form, composition, traceability and lot acceptance must be agreed with the intended customer, then demonstrated repeatedly. See the purity and commodity-flow section for published examples. [S95](https://store.astm.org/b0092_b0092m-17r25.html) [S97](https://luxfermagtech.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Luxfer-Data-Sheets-Pure-Magnesium-Powders-for-Commercial-Applications-7.pdf) [S98](https://magretech.us/products/az91d/) [S54](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/magnesium-statistics-and-information)

Magrathea’s Wogen partnership is evidence of a future route to market, with placement targeted up to 7,000 t/y by 2029. It is not evidence of current shipments or a guaranteed volume for an unrelated U.S. developer. Named distributor relationships, nonbinding interest, product qualification and enforceable purchase commitments represent different levels of commercial evidence. [S59](https://www.magratheametals.com/news/wogen)

### The economic test is the whole plant

Compare delivered cost per kilogram of accepted product: prepared feed + electricity and direct heat + reagents and consumables + labor and maintenance + waste handling + capital recovery + freight, less only defensible coproduct credits. Cell energy and the purchase price of raw brine are incomplete cost boundaries. Historical process inventories show why preparation and utilities must stay inside the assessment. [S111](https://publications.anl.gov/anlpubs/2014/09/107574.pdf)

Illustrative sensitivity, not observed plant performance or a power-price quote: assume total metered plant electricity of 15 kWh/kg saleable Mg and electricity at $60/MWh. Electricity alone costs 15 × 60 / 1,000 = $0.90/kg. At the same consumption, each $10/MWh change shifts that component by $0.15/kg. These analyst-selected inputs say nothing about the other costs, direct fuel, the negotiated tariff or the final metal selling price.

For a chloride plant, chlorine handling or sale needs a credible local solution; coproduct revenue should not be assumed without evidence of a buyer and usable product quality. Compare the proposed plant with an incumbent serving the same destination, product specification and contract terms. Regional price assessments have different delivery, duty, grade and lot conventions. Their differences are not automatically an available producer margin. [S21](https://www.confer.cz/metal/2016/download/1804-aluminium-and-magnesium-production-changes-and-perspectives.pdf) [S100](https://www.spglobal.com/content/dam/spglobal/ci/en/documents/platts/en/our-methodology/methodology-specifications/metals/nonferrous-specifications.pdf) [S101](https://www.metal.com/magnesium/202105250002)

Environmental comparison needs the same discipline: report both direct plant performance and a feed-to-metal life-cycle boundary. Include the actual power supply, upstream reagents, direct heat, recovery losses and treatment of coproducts. A low-carbon electricity contract could improve the case, but neither an emissions advantage nor a cost advantage is demonstrated by this report. [S111](https://publications.anl.gov/anlpubs/2014/09/107574.pdf)

### A practical development objective

Current projects make the opportunity tangible while keeping its maturity in perspective. Arkansas Magnesium has feed-linked sample and future distribution evidence. Big Blue’s August 2026 construction update targets first demonstration metal in January 2027; its DOE project seeks a sustained run of an alternative thermal route. Neither is counted here as established U.S. annual primary supply. The project directory records each separately. [S58](https://www.magratheametals.com/news/arkamagastm) [S59](https://www.magratheametals.com/news/wogen) [S117](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/mining/project-selections-foa-3105-critical-material-innovation-efficiency-and-alternatives) [S118](https://bigbluetech.com/commercial-demo/)

1. **Choose feed and product together.** Identify a domestic feed supplier and an initial product specification before optimizing the cell. Establish which impurities and preparation costs control that pairing.

2. **Prove the integrated balance.** Measure feed preparation, extraction, collection, refining, accepted product and residues in one campaign. Demonstrate stability with representative feed variation.

3. **Run customer qualification alongside scale-up.** Produce repeated trial lots, obtain buyer feedback, and test the cost model using measured recovery, energy and component replacement.

4. **Advance only when the supply chain closes.** Tie a continuous demonstration to credible feed, power, waste handling and purchase commitments. Select commercial scale from the supported market and plant performance.

The proposed objective is dependable U.S. primary magnesium from a documented feed chain, with repeatable buyer-qualified output and competitive delivered cost. Electrochemical development is well motivated because it builds on a proven metal-extraction principle and can address domestic chloride or oxide resources. The decisive innovation must be demonstrated across the entire feed-to-product system.

## Methods and gaps

### Scope and cutoff

Research cutoff: 27 September 2026. This is a public-source desk review of major established primary producers, selected recyclers/alloy makers, and separately identified development projects. It is not a census of every Chinese smelter or every global scrap remelter. “Latest located” means latest year-qualified figure in the reviewed evidence, not a claim that no newer private or inaccessible disclosure exists.

### What counts as primary metal

Primary production is new metallic magnesium extracted from mineral/brine/residue feed. Recovery from old industrial residues may be described as recycling by a developer, but is separated here from remelting already metallic scrap. Magnesium oxide, hydroxide, chloride, carbonate, potash and dolomite tonnage are excluded from metal totals. Alloy output includes other elements; shipments may include trading and inventory movements.

### Avoiding double counting

Do not add a group to its subsidiaries, primary metal to the alloys subsequently made from it, a regional total to its firms, or recycled metal to primary supply. UKTMP/AVISMA internal MgCl2 regeneration can repeatedly circulate the same magnesium. Gross electrolytic output, merchant ingot and virgin primary supply are different measures. Project nameplates and inactive plant capacities are excluded from current production.

### Quantity conventions

t means metric tonnes; t/y means metric tonnes per year. Numbers in source tables reported in thousand tonnes are multiplied by 1,000. Approximate values and estimates retain their labels. Annual actuals, national estimates, sales, partial-year shipments and historical capacities are shown on separate bases. Unknown values remain blank in numeric exports, never imputed as zero. USGS dashes mean zero and are displayed as such; they are different from unknown company quantities.

### Feedstock analytical basis

Report Mg, MgO and MgCl2 as stated; do not silently convert among them. Raw ore/brine, dolime (calcined dolomite), prepared chloride, scrap alloy and final metal are distinct sampling boundaries. Dry/wet basis, impurities and preparation are retained when disclosed; otherwise marked unavailable. No theoretical stoichiometric ore requirement is represented as an observed industrial consumption rate.

### Process names

Silicothermic means using silicon, normally supplied in ferrosilicon, to remove oxygen and release magnesium. Aluminothermic uses aluminum instead. These materials are called reductants. Pidgeon names a thermal route; VAMI identifies the electrolytic technology used at Dead Sea Magnesium. Facility-specific explanations follow the feed through preparation, metal production and finishing.

### Customers and applications

A named OEM at the end of a component chain is not automatically an ingot buyer. Current deliveries, historical relationships, internal consumption, development partnerships, future offtake and nonbinding LOIs are identified separately. General industry applications are context, not a producer-specific sales allocation. [S65](https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/publications/more/responsible-raw-materials-report-2025-3229/download?disposition=attachment)

### Source hierarchy and access

Preference is given to filings, company operational disclosures, government reports, permits and technical references. Industry reporting fills status gaps; historical conferences and directories are clearly dated. Mirrors and search-index-only access are identified in the source register. Sources are linked, not archived; no private customer data or confidential annual production is assumed.

### Why there is no current producer ranking

Company magnesium-only annual volumes are sparse. Chinese private producers often disclose only capacities; older tables do not prove current operations. Baowu’s consolidated nonferrous tonnage includes aluminum. REMT publishes product sales. Russian and Kazakh figures require reconciliation with titanium loops. Even national series conflict: Baowu cites CNIA’s China 2025 primary output of 1,093,500 t, versus USGS’s 950,000 t estimate. The CNIA figure is not inserted into USGS’s world denominator. [S01](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-magnesium-metal.pdf) [S02](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225223859.PDF) [S15](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0430/2026043000040_c.pdf) [S33](https://bizup.kz/ru/documents/p2500000339)

Priority gaps are dated company primary output, current commissioning/utilization of Chinese expansions, mine-linked feed assays, purchased/captive splits and named customer tonnage.
