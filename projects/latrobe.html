Project profile / Separate from operating supply

Latrobe Magnesium

Hazelwood North, Victoria; proposed US and Malaysian options

Development / demonstration evidence. Excluded from established commercial primary production totals.

Company, site and status

ASX-listed developer. FY2026 report describes MgO demonstration and a metal circuit under installation, with first plant metal targeted for H2 2026. No sustained commercial metal annual output verified. S56

Feedstock and source

Demonstration: EnergyAustralia Yallourn brown-coal ash, purchased under amended supply arrangements. Screening, drying and consistency matter; wet/variable ash disrupted initial operation. Raw ash Mg assay not verified. US option: Société Le Nickel (Eramet) ferronickel slag, stated ~33% MgO, under a company-described 20-year binding MOU; analytical moisture basis undisclosed. S56 S57

Proposed or demonstrated route

Screen/condition residue → acid-based hydrometallurgical recovery of Mg-rich oxide → prepare briquettes with reductant/lime → thermal reduction/refining → intended metal. Demonstrated MgO is an intermediate, not magnesium metal. S55 S57

Actual versus design scale

FY2026 report specifies initial Phase 1B metal equipment of 500 t/y. The September presentation describes 500 t/y crude crowns followed by 500 t/y refined ingots in Phase 1C: sequential stages of the same metal, not 1,000 t/y combined. The older headline demonstration design of 1,000 t/y is not treated as available capacity. Proposed US first train 50,000 t/y; MgO campaign ~20 t excluded from metal totals. S56 S93 S55

Future buyers and applications

Metal Exchange: future distribution/offtake and prepayment; no proven current metal shipments. US project LOIs: Metal Exchange ≥25,000 t/y, Meridian Lightweight Technologies 5,000 and Twin Cities 2,000; LOIs are not firm realized sales. S56 S57

Unresolved dependencies

Ash mining/licence arrangements, feed conditioning, financing and commissioning remain dependencies. No verified feed-to-metal ratio. Project forecasts from different releases are not added.
