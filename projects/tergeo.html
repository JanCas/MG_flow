Project profile / Separate from operating supply

Tergeo / former Alliance Magnesium

Val-des-Sources / Danville area, Québec, Canada

Development / demonstration evidence. Excluded from established commercial primary production totals.

Company, site and status

Former magnesium site entering municipal redevelopment as 4M2, following insolvency. May 2026 municipal reporting identifies the former Tergeo assets with the new industrial district; an agreement with Exterra supports redevelopment. No commercial primary restart verified. S62 S73 S74

Feedstock and source

Historical proposal: serpentine tailings for new metal, separate from scrap remelting. The redevelopment page reports access to more than 110 million t of characterized mineral material; this is a stock/resource claim, not magnesium tonnage or annual feed. Assays, prepared chloride purity and future Mg feed rights remain unresolved. S63 S73

Proposed or demonstrated route

Proposed tailings processing → purified magnesium chloride → electrolysis/refining; separate scrap remelting/alloy operations. Recycling startup is not proof that tailings-to-primary-metal production operated commercially. S63

Actual versus design scale

No reliable current annual primary actual or available nameplate established; pre-insolvency targets excluded from operating capacity. S62

Future buyers and applications

No current offtake or buyer relationship verified after insolvency.

Unresolved dependencies

Municipal redevelopment establishes a new direction for the assets. Exact final asset/title allocation, any future magnesium operator, feed rights and a metal restart schedule remain unverified.
