Project profile / Separate from operating supply

MPI Magnesium / Mineral Proiect Invest

Rășinari–Săliște, Sibiu County, Romania

Development / demonstration evidence. Excluded from established commercial primary production totals.

Company, site and status

Compound-first development by Romanian-owned Mineral Proiect Invest. September 2026 licence is a development milestone. Updated FAQ makes metal only a possible Phase 3, with no selected technology; it is not an operating metal project. S64 S77

Feedstock and source

Magnesium-bearing serpentinite from the licensed Valea Dobra perimeter near Rășinari; initial crushing at the quarry and planned processing at Săliște. Ore assay, impurities and prepared-feed specifications not located. S64 S77

Proposed or demonstrated route

Initial phases target magnesium oxide, hydroxide and then fused magnesia, all compounds. A possible later metal route remains undecided; Pidgeon is not the process of the proposed Phase 1 compound plant. S77

Actual versus design scale

No magnesium-metal annual actual or verified operating metal capacity. Compound quantities must not enter metal totals. S77

Future buyers and applications

No named magnesium-metal buyer verified. Compound applications described by the developer do not demonstrate future metal sales.

Unresolved dependencies

Mining, environmental and building approvals are separate. The metal phase remains conditional and must not be treated as licensed or commissioned by the mineral licence alone. S77
