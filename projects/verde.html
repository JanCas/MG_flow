Project profile / Separate from operating supply

Verde Magnesium

Budureasa mine/planned smelter; proposed Mother Plant at Beiuș, Bihor, Romania

Development / demonstration evidence. Excluded from established commercial primary production totals.

Company, site and status

Proposed primary producer backed by sponsor Amerocap. A May 2026 CEO interview describes continuing mine and pilot development, not sustained commercial metal production. S75 S76

Feedstock and source

Captive Budureasa brucite-bearing ore. The IMA deck claims deposit MgO above 22%; its pilot diagram uses ore at 24.18% MgO and a separated concentrate at 60–69% MgO. Moisture basis and impurities are unspecified. Its T2 illustration shows 11.4 t ore → 3.4 t concentrate → 2.2 t MgO, then 0.8 t aluminum powder added per 1 t metal. This is a company pilot illustration, not verified commercial consumption or a complete mass balance. Aluminum supplier/acceptance grade undisclosed. S75

Proposed or demonstrated route

Separate/upgrade brucite-bearing ore → calcine to oxide → prepare charge with aluminum reductant → aluminothermic reduction → refine/cast metal. Aluminum scrap is intended as a reductant; mineral upgrading is distinct from metal-scrap remelting. S75 S76

Actual versus design scale

May 2026 CEO interview: 360 t/y Mother Plant followed by up to 30,000 t/y smelter by 2030; quarry restart targeted for end-2026. These are forecasts. The older 90,000 t/y by 2036 ambition is retained only as a historical target, not added capacity. S76 S61

Future buyers and applications

Industrial EU/US markets intended. The May 2026 interview says firm offtake negotiations remain relevant to accelerating the larger smelter; no named binding customer or delivered annual volume verified. S76

Unresolved dependencies

Financing, resource recertification, permitting, qualification and commissioning remain dependencies. Pilot diagrams do not establish steady-state commercial recovery.
