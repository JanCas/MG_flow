Project profile / Separate from operating supply

Big Blue Technologies — Cheyenne demonstration

Cheyenne, Wyoming, United States

Development / demonstration evidence. Excluded from established commercial primary production totals.

Company, site and status

Development facility; detailed ownership not disclosed in the reviewed sources. The August 2026 update reports the building completed and major equipment sourced and ordered; first metal is targeted for January 2027. Garrison Minerals is a site partner. S118

Feedstock and source

DOE describes raw dolomite plus aluminum scrap. Separately, a 2025 Letter of Understanding concerns purchased MgO from Canadian supplier Baymag. These descriptions do not establish the demonstration plant’s final feed mix. Quarry, MgO assay, aluminum-scrap chemistry and preparation losses are undisclosed. Purchased MgO is already an upgraded intermediate, not raw ore. S117 S120

Proposed or demonstrated route

Aluminothermic reduction: prepare magnesium-bearing feed and aluminum reductant → extract and purify magnesium → cast ingot, with calcium aluminate coproduct. This is a thermal chemical-reduction route; electricity use does not make it electrolysis. Detailed feed preparation and current consumption ratios are not disclosed. S117 S118

Actual versus design scale

No verified commercial annual output. Company pilot results claim magnesium purity above 99.9%. Latest demonstration design: three smelters totaling at least 200 tons/year, with “tons” left undefined by the company; not converted to metric tonnes. DOE’s 2,000-hour continuous-run objective is a future demonstration target. Calcium aluminate is excluded from magnesium-metal quantities. S119 S118 S117

Future buyers and applications

No named magnesium customer or delivered customer tonnage located in the reviewed disclosures. Baymag is an input supplier and Garrison a site partner; neither relationship establishes a magnesium-metal purchase. DOE identifies potential alloying, lightweight-part and metallurgical uses, not contracted buyers. S120 S118 S117

Unresolved dependencies

Commissioning, sustained recovery, energy/reductant consumption, consistent product qualification and customer commitments remain to be demonstrated. Final feed contracts and permitted commercial operating conditions were not established by the reviewed announcements.
