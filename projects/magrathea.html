Project profile / Separate from operating supply

Magrathea / Arkansas Magnesium with TETRA

Arkansas project at TETRA Evergreen campus; separate San Francisco Bay Area pilot

Development / demonstration evidence. Excluded from established commercial primary production totals.

Company, site and status

Developer/technical demonstration. The JV operating agreement established Arkansas Magnesium in March 2026; Phase 1 engineering followed. June metal samples from TETRA brine do not establish annual commercial output. S71 S58

Feedstock and source

Arkansas: TETRA Smackover brine supplies magnesium-bearing chloride. Separately, Cargill agreed to supply San Francisco Bay sea-salt magnesium co-products for pilot scale-up in 2024. These feed chains are distinct. Raw-brine assays, impurity panels and prepared-feed specifications were not located. S58 S72

Proposed or demonstrated route

Prepare and purify brine-derived chloride feed → proprietary electrolysis → refine metal. Product purity above 99.9% is not feed grade. S58

Actual versus design scale

No verified commercial annual output or operating nameplate. January 2026 announcement targets market placement of up to 7,000 t/y by 2029; a target is not realized output. S59

Future buyers and applications

Wogen’s January agreement covers future distribution from Arkansas Phase 1/2 and working capital. The company also reports pre-sales to an unnamed Detroit Big 3 automaker; no identity or current annual deliveries inferred. Nonbinding demand indications are not production capacity. S59

Unresolved dependencies

Scale-up, power, feed purification, plant financing and commercial commissioning remain dependencies. JV formation is confirmed; no public feed-to-product ratio located.
