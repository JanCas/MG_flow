Ust-Kamenogorsk Titanium and Magnesium Plant (UKTMP)

Öskemen (Ust-Kamenogorsk), East Kazakhstan

Primary + internal magnesium regenerationCommercial integrated titanium/magnesium plant

Company and facilities

Ownership
JSC UKTMP, KASE-listed issuer; ultimate controlling ownership not established from the reviewed production sources. S34
Main products
Magnesium reducing agent, merchant ingots and titanium products

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Purchased/prepared carnallite feed and internally returned MgCl2 from titanium reduction. Virgin mineral input and recycled chloride are separate magnesium sources.
Source and purchasing
External carnallite mine/supplier not disclosed in the reviewed technical reference. Titanium mineral resources cannot be substituted as the source of magnesium. Internal MgCl2 arises when magnesium reduces titanium chloride. S32 S33
Grade and impurities
Prepared anhydrous carnallite: MgCl2 ≥50% (grade A) or ≥48% (B), MgO ≤0.80%, carbon ≤0.08%. These are percentages of the prepared salt, not elemental Mg or mined ore grade. S33
Consumption and boundaries
No defensible virgin-feed/primary-metal factor derived from gross plant throughput because the internal chloride loop is substantial.
Supply dependencies
Electricity and external chloride makeup support an integrated titanium cycle; supplier identity and present makeup availability undisclosed.

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Prepare/dehydrate chloride feed → electrolyse to magnesium → refine → mostly return metal to titanium reduction, with some merchant ingots. MgCl2 generated in titanium production is returned for electrolysis; this is internal regeneration, not new virgin metal each cycle. S32 S33 The 2025 technical reference describes melting/chlorination/settling during final carnallite preparation, then metal refining and grade/melt identification for merchant ingot. This prepared-feed chain is distinct from the returned MgCl2 stream. S33

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
Annual process-stream ranges reported for 2021–2022: crude Mg 19,043–19,568 t; reducing-agent Mg 16,918–18,236 t; merchant MG90 ingots 108–560 t. Individual years cannot be assigned reliably; these are not two-year totals. S33
Capacity, t/y
USGS Kazakhstan 21,000 t/y is a national estimate; no independently reconciled current plant primary-only nameplate. S01
Interpretation and recent trend
These overlapping process streams are not additive. The 2025 audited-financial notice provides no physical output; national 13,000 t estimated primary production is not a UKTMP audited actual. S01 S34

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
Internal titanium production is the documented consumer. External magnesium-ingot buyers are undisclosed. S32
Intermediate products
Mg reducing agent → titanium sponge → titanium products
Final applications
Titanium customer aerospace applications are downstream of titanium, not direct magnesium-metal sales.
Relationship basis
Current internal consumption

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
Recent primary-only output, fresh versus regenerated feed split and current merchant sales are unavailable.
