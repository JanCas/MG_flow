Solikamsk Magnesium Works (SMZ)

Solikamsk, Perm Krai

Primary; integrated chemicalsCommercial producer; 2026 upgrade constraints reported

Company and facilities

Ownership
Rosatom mining division affiliation confirmed by company news. S27
Main products
Primary magnesium/alloys; associated chlorine/rare-metal chemical products excluded

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Carnallite chloride mineral; dried/dehydrated salt is the prepared electrolysis feed.
Source and purchasing
Historical supply from Uralkali’s Verkhnekamskoye carnallite operations. Uralkali’s 2024 report confirms Solikamsk-1 carnallite mining/processing, but a present SMZ purchase contract was not found. Feed is historically purchased, not a demonstrated SMZ-owned mine. S28 S29
Grade and impurities
No current contracted raw-carnallite assay or electrolyser-feed impurity specification verified.
Consumption and boundaries
No annual feed-to-metal consumption boundary located; co-production of chlorine complicates allocation.
Supply dependencies
SMM reported equipment upgrades constraining output in March 2026. Its monthly signal is not annualized. Historical dependence on external carnallite remains a supply-chain consideration, not proof of current shortage. S30

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Prepare/dehydrate carnallite → molten-salt electrolysis → refine and cast magnesium/alloys. Chlorine supports the integrated chemical chain. Company confirms electrolytic magnesium manufacture. S27 S29 A February 2026 company repair announcement confirms carnallite dehydration within the magnesium-electrolysis department. S89

Read the detailed production-process guide ↗

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
Latest recent annual production unavailable. 15,336 t in 2019 is reported sales, not production. S31
Capacity, t/y
18,200 t/y historical capacity in an industry compilation; current available nameplate not verified. S31
Interpretation and recent trend
Company’s 1,250,000 t milestone is cumulative production since 1936, reached February 2025; not a yearly quantity. Russia’s 60,000 t 2025 national estimate cannot be assigned to SMZ. S27 S01

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
Current metal buyers undisclosed; internal chlorine use in rare-metal processing is documented historically. S29
Intermediate products
Mg → ingot/alloy; chlorine → internal chemical processing
Final applications
No source-backed named final metal customer allocation.
Relationship basis
External buyers undisclosed

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
Current output, feed contract and grade, effective capacity and the Russian national/plant statistical reconciliation remain gaps.
