Company and facilities

Ownership
Private Turkish company; ultimate ownership not verified in reviewed sources. S35 S37
Main products
Primary magnesium ingot

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Dolomite and ferrosilicon-based reductant; prepared feed is calcined, ground and pelletized material.
Source and purchasing
National inventory documents dolomite mining in the process chain; specific supplying quarry, contract and internal/purchased share not disclosed. Do not confuse similarly named decorative-stone businesses with this feed source. S35
Grade and impurities
No facility-specific raw or calcined feed assay or impurity limits located.
Consumption and boundaries
No supported t dolomite/t Mg factor located.
Supply dependencies
No current documented company-specific feed disruption; mineral, reductant and energy sources remain material disclosure gaps.

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Mine/crush dolomite → calcine, grind and pelletize reduction feed → Pidgeon silicothermic reduction → refine and cast magnesium. The national inventory specifically identifies Kar Mineral and production since 2016. S35

Read the detailed production-process guide ↗

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
No company annual actual located; Türkiye’s 2025 estimated output 15,000 t is a national proxy. S01
Capacity, t/y
15,000 t/y in the USGS plant/country listing. S36
Interpretation and recent trend
The government’s sole-producer identification supports association with national supply, but an estimate remains different from company-reported actual production. ESAN is not added as a second current producer. S35
