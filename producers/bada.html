Shanxi Bada Magnesium

Bailin, Guojiazhuang, Wenxi, Shanxi

Company and facilities

Ownership
Company describes affiliation with Meridian / Wanfeng Auto Holding. The affiliation alone does not establish an ingot offtake contract. S11
Main products
Magnesium ingot, alloys and downstream castings

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Dolomite from an associated mine; calcined dolomite is the prepared mineral feed. Reductant sourcing is not detailed.
Source and purchasing
Company claims a dolomite mine about 8 km from the plant and resources around 200 million t. This supports captive provenance; no current mine output or audited reserve classification provided. S11
Grade and impurities
No numerical feed assay or impurity limits located. Advertised high purity must not be converted into a numerical ore grade.
Consumption and boundaries
No supported dolomite or reductant consumption per tonne of metal.
Supply dependencies
Captive nearby mineral source is documented; current external reductant and energy supply terms are undisclosed.

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Dolomite quarrying/preparation → calcination and thermal reduction → refining/alloying → ingots and cast components. The mineral-to-product chain is described commercially; current retort configuration is not verified. S11

Read the detailed production-process guide ↗

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
29,800 t primary Mg in 2017. Company also advertises 35,000 t/year without a reporting year; this is not a dated actual. S05 S11
Capacity, t/y
40,000 t/y company claim versus 50,000 t/y in the historical USGS industry table; discrepancy unresolved. S11 S14
Interpretation and recent trend
The 35,000 t/year statement is shown only as an undated operating claim. No defensible 2025 company actual located. A January 2026 visit confirms integrated mining, smelting, processing and recycling activity. Its 150,000 t/y mixed-product design claim is not substituted for primary capacity. S84

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
Affiliated Meridian is a downstream automotive fabricator. Named OEMs in group marketing are not verified direct Bada metal buyers. S11
Intermediate products
Alloy → motor and gearbox housings; exact internal metal transfer undisclosed
Final applications
2026 reporting supports new-energy-vehicle castings sold to unnamed vehicle manufacturers. S10
Relationship basis
Affiliation and sector evidence; direct metal customers undisclosed

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
Current annual primary output, capacity reconciliation, ore assay and direct commercial relationships need confirmation.
