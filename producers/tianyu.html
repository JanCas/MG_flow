Company and facilities

Ownership
Privately operated group; current beneficial ownership not established in reviewed sources. S06
Main products
Magnesium ingot and alloys; integrated ferrosilicon and semi-coke

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Dolomite, ferrosilicon and fuel gas associated with the coal/semi-coke complex.
Source and purchasing
Fugu industrial cluster. Specific dolomite quarry, current supplier and proportion of internally supplied ferrosilicon are undisclosed. Do not treat regional coal mining as magnesium mineral supply. S06 S07
Grade and impurities
No source-matched ore or calcined-feed assay located; no numerical impurities disclosed.
Consumption and boundaries
No annual mine-to-metal or charge-to-metal mass balance located.
Supply dependencies
The chairman described dolomite mining restrictions, transport and ferrosilicon availability/cost constraints in 2019. These are historical dependencies, not proof of current interruption. S07

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Dolomite preparation and calcination → dolime/ferrosilicon charge → silicothermic magnesium production → refining and alloy casting. Commercial integration is documented; a current facility process audit was not located. S06 S07

The 2017 quantity is retained for traceability and excluded from any 2025 ranking. Xiyuan and Tianyu are not separately added without a consolidation boundary.
