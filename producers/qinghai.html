Company and facilities

Ownership
Qinghai Salt Lake Magnesium was upstream supplier; Magontec Qinghai was a separate downstream alloy operation. S45 S47
Main products
Historical electrolytic magnesium and alloy products

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Magnesium-rich salt-lake brine/chloride intermediate; purified, dehydrated chloride feeds electrolysis.
Source and purchasing
Qarhan salt-lake resource at Golmud. Magontec bought/received primary liquid magnesium for alloy conversion; that does not make it a second primary smelter. S47
Grade and impurities
A 2025 trial reports prepared molten MgCl2 feed with MgO below 0.06%; this is an impurity in the prepared melt, not Mg content of lake brine. No current raw-brine assay or full impurity panel located. S80
Consumption and boundaries
Trial melting/purification reports 99.12% MgCl2 recovery. This is not a fresh-brine-to-metal yield; different campaign periods prevent a defensible overall feed/product ratio. S80
Supply dependencies
Magontec’s 2025 report records the Qinghai operation’s 2024 production cessation and entity closure in May 2025. Assets transferred to QSLM; the report alone does not prove permanent closure of all upstream electrolysis. S45 A 2025 trial reports resolution of excess nickel in product; later commercialization still requires process scale-up. S80

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Brine/chloride preparation → dehydration and electrolysis at the upstream complex → liquid magnesium → former Magontec alloy casthouse → alloy products. S47 A 2025 one-cell trial separately documented chloride melting/purification before electrolysis, including MgO below 0.06% in prepared molten MgCl2. Trial performance does not establish continuous whole-site operation. S80

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
No annual whole-site actual located. The 4# electrolysis cell trial produced 292.705 t liquid Mg from 6 February to 30 April 2025; 265 t ingot was cast from the same campaign and must not be added. S80
Capacity, t/y
100,000 t/y primary-metal project design scale, explicitly named in a supplier’s historical contract. July 2026 reporting claims design operation but gives no annual actual or clear boundary. Historical 56,000 t/y Magontec alloy casthouse is a separate conversion step, not added primary capacity. S94 S85 S47
Interpretation and recent trend
The 2025 one-cell trial is not annual output. July 2026 provincial reporting claims design operation of the 100,000 t/y project, without a dated annual-output table. September Minmetals reporting describes expanded-trial closeout and pilot construction within the integrated project. These different scopes remain unreconciled; neither universal shutdown nor full annual utilization is inferred. S85 S81

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
Magontec Qinghai was a documented historical internal-chain counterparty; that relationship has ended. S45 S47
Intermediate products
Liquid primary Mg → alloy ingot at former casthouse
Final applications
Historical alloy markets; no current final buyer established.
Relationship basis
Historical supply relationship, ended

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
Whole-site operating rate, annual primary output, ownership allocation and current customers remain unresolved; Magontec’s exit does not prove upstream metal cessation.
