Company and facilities

Ownership
ICL subsidiary; do not repeat the historical Volkswagen joint-venture ownership as current. S18 S19
Main products
Pure magnesium and alloys; magnesium chloride is a separate compound business

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Dead Sea brine is the raw resource. Carnallite recovered by the integrated Dead Sea Works evaporation system is the magnesium-rich intermediate; the electrolyser requires prepared, dehydrated chloride feed.
Source and purchasing
Internally integrated supply from the northern Dead Sea basin through Dead Sea Works ponds and carnallite harvesting. No outside mine is required for this principal magnesium feed. S18 S20
Grade and impurities
No current plant feed Mg concentration or impurity specification located. ICL’s approximately 20% KCl figure describes harvested potash-bearing material, not elemental Mg grade. S18
Consumption and boundaries
No public, boundary-consistent brine-volume or carnallite-mass consumption per tonne of magnesium found.
Supply dependencies
Continuous power, pond salt harvesting and reliable brine access underpin production. Concession expiry in 2030 and regional logistics/security are disclosed risks. S18

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Evaporate brine and harvest carnallite → prepare/dehydrate chloride salts → VAMI-type molten-salt electrolysis → refine magnesium and alloy/cast saleable metal. Chlorine participates in the wider integrated chemical complex. S20 S21

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
Approximately 17,800 t magnesium metal in 2025, company reported. S18
Capacity, t/y
34,000 t/y Israel capacity in USGS 2026 versus 21,000 t/y in its older country structure data; plant-boundary/date discrepancy unresolved. S01 S22
Interpretation and recent trend
Company metal output increased from approximately 17,000 t in 2024 to 17,800 t in 2025, about 5% (calculated from rounded values). The 2025 company figure takes priority over USGS’s 20,000 t national estimate. The separate 112,000 t solid magnesium chloride figure is excluded. S19 S18 S01 The August 2026 quarterly release was checked; it does not provide a newer annual magnesium-metal quantity. S91

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
Individual buyers not disclosed. ICL identifies US and Brazilian markets and aluminum, steel and automotive customer sectors. S18
Intermediate products
Mg ingot → aluminum alloying or magnesium alloys / steel treatment
Final applications
Company-supported aluminum products, automotive uses and steelmaking; no named OEM allocation. S18
Relationship basis
Current sectors and regions; buyers undisclosed

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
Capacity reconciliation, full feed assay/ratio and buyer identities remain unavailable.
