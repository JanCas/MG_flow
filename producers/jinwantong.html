Production process

Route at the covered facility
A February 2026 Fugu report identifies a Jinwantong silicothermic Mg99.95A demonstration line in stable trial operation. In this thermal route, prepared oxide feed is reduced with a silicon-bearing charge, then metal is condensed/refined. The site’s full feed-preparation specification and other-line status remain unverified. S88
