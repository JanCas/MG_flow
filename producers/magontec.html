Magontec

Bottrop, Germany; Sântana, Romania; Xi’an, China (anodes)

Company and facilities

Ownership
Australian-listed Magontec Limited; do not retain the former Qinghai casthouse as a current group operation. S45
Main products
Recycled and specialist magnesium alloys, distributed primary alloys and magnesium anodes; titanium electronic anodes excluded from Mg totals

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Industrial magnesium diecasting scrap; purchased primary alloy/metal; alloying additions. These are metal-bearing inputs, not virgin mineral ore.
Source and purchasing
European automotive diecasters return scrap to Bottrop/Sântana. A new, unnamed Chinese primary-alloy supplier supported 2025 distribution; no supplier identity is inferred from the phrase “world’s largest.” S45 S47
Grade and impurities
Feed is alloy-dependent; lot-level Mg content, oils/oxides and acceptance limits are not disclosed. Product alloy labels are not assays of the total scrap stream.
Consumption and boundaries
The 2024 report describes 40–50% of diecaster input returning as production scrap. That is a caster-loop fraction, not recycler metal loss or virgin-feed yield. S47
Supply dependencies
Automotive casting activity controls scrap availability. Following Qinghai exit, external primary-alloy supply is needed to service customers. S45

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Collect and segregate production scrap → remelt/clean and adjust chemistry → cast reusable alloy → return to diecasters. Separately buy/distribute primary alloy and manufacture sacrificial anodes. This is recycling/alloy conversion, not new primary extraction. S45 S47

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
Annual recycling output not located. About 700 t primary-alloy shipments in H2 2025 were distributed product, not own primary production. S45 The H1 2026 report provides business-volume growth percentages but no usable new annual tonnage. S67
Capacity, t/y
24,000 t/y European recycling capacity disclosed in 2024. S46
Interpretation and recent trend
H1 2026 versus H1 2025: total Metals volumes +77%, standard-alloy sales +33%, primary-alloy trading +653%, specialist-alloy volumes +107%. These overlap and cannot be added; trading is not own primary production. S67

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
European automotive diecasters; unnamed aerospace customers for specialist alloys, with North America the largest regional specialist market. Primary supplier remains unnamed. S67
Intermediate products
Recycled alloy → automotive castings; specialist alloys → aerospace parts; Mg anodes → corrosion protection
Final applications
Automotive lightweight components, aerospace alloys and water-heater magnesium anodes. Growth in titanium electronic anodes is excluded from magnesium volume. S45 S67
Relationship basis
Current sectors; supplier/customer names undisclosed

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
Annual recycled tonnage, primary top-up share, scrap chemistry and named current counterparties are unavailable.
