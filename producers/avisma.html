Company and facilities

Ownership
VSMPO-AVISMA group; current controlling ownership not resolved in the selected process sources.
Main products
Magnesium as titanium reductant; associated titanium products

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Historically carnallite and internally recycled magnesium chloride from titanium production.
Source and purchasing
Historical Uralkali carnallite supply is documented in court records. Current mine/supplier terms and fresh-versus-returned chloride split are undisclosed. S44
Grade and impurities
No current feed assay or prepared-electrolyte specification verified.
Consumption and boundaries
No valid virgin-feed-to-primary-metal ratio established.
Supply dependencies
A 2026 industry bulletin reports cessation of primary smelting. This does not establish closure of the entire titanium complex or cessation of internal regeneration. S30

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Prepare chloride feed → electrolyse magnesium → use magnesium in titanium-chloride reduction → recycle resulting MgCl2. Gross circulating metal is not necessarily new primary supply. S44

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
No recent verified annual primary output located.
Capacity, t/y
Current primary-only nameplate not verified.
Interpretation and recent trend
Do not assign the balance of Russia’s national total to AVISMA or count repeated regenerated magnesium as new primary metal.

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
Internal titanium production is historically documented; titanium customers are not automatically magnesium customers. S44
Intermediate products
Mg → titanium sponge reduction → titanium products
Final applications
Titanium manufacturing, not a directly traced magnesium component market.
Relationship basis
Internal historical chain; current metal status uncertain

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
