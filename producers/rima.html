Company and facilities

Ownership
Privately held Brazilian group founded by Ricardo Vicintin; precise current shareholding not established here. S25
Main products
Primary ingot, magnesium alloys, powder and cast products

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Dolomite plus ferrosilicon. Prepared reduction mix reported as about 85% calcined dolomite and 15% ferrosilicon; reductant contains about 75% Si. Quartz, charcoal and iron-bearing materials used for ferrosilicon are auxiliary upstream inputs, not magnesium ore. S23
Source and purchasing
Company reports its own high-purity dolomite supply; exact mine name and numerical assay were not located. Ferrosilicon is produced within RIMA’s integrated operations. S23 S26
Grade and impurities
Dolomite assay undisclosed. The 75% Si figure is reductant composition; minimum 99.8% Mg refers to saleable raw metal, not ore. S23 S24
Consumption and boundaries
85:15 is prepared-charge mass composition; it is not 85 t ore per 15 t metal and does not yield a mine-to-metal consumption factor. No such factor disclosed. S23 The same notice reverses the respective percentages in stage iv; the explicit 85% dolime / 15% FeSi statement comes from stage i. S23
Supply dependencies
Captive mineral/reductant integration is documented. Government review identifies RIMA as the entire domestic primary raw-magnesium industry for Apr 2025–Mar 2026, but withholds production tonnage. S23

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Calcine dolomite → grind/briquette the oxide feed with ferrosilicon and silicon reductants → proprietary RIMA vacuum silicothermic reduction → condense magnesium crystals → refine and cast ingot/alloy, make powder or feed component production. SECEX’s September 2026 description of Bocaiúva specifies internal heating around 1,200 °C for approximately 10 hours before crystal recovery, with heat recovery between charge columns. These are described process conditions, not an annual throughput or recovery guarantee. S23 S24

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
No dated company annual actual located. USGS Brazil 2025 estimate: 20,000 t, a national proxy only. S01 S23
Capacity, t/y
22,000 t/y national capacity, associated with the sole Brazilian primary producer. S01 S23
Interpretation and recent trend
Founder’s July 2026 interview says 22,000 t/year; reporting period/basis unspecified, so treated as a run-rate claim rather than 2025 actual. S25

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
Named current metal buyers undisclosed. Embraer is described in product-development work; this is not verified serial offtake. S25
Intermediate products
Ingot → alloys/castings; powder → metallurgical treatment
Final applications
Company-supported transport castings and steel treatment; Embraer relationship is development-stage. S23 S25
Relationship basis
Current sectors; named development partner

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
Mine identity/assay, annual company actual, external buyer allocation and mine-to-metal ratio remain undisclosed.
