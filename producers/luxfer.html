Company and facilities

Ownership
Luxfer MEL Technologies is within Luxfer Holdings PLC’s Elektron segment. A Wynnchurch-backed buyer agreed to acquire the parent in July 2026; the September filing still describes outstanding closing conditions. Completion is not established by this review. S68 S69 S70
Main products
Specialty magnesium alloys and finished parts; other chemical products excluded

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Purchased magnesium/alloying inputs and returned machining chips, swarf and turnings. Group alloy recipes use rare-earth metals including yttrium, gadolinium and neodymium; this does not identify a specific batch or supplier. S68
Source and purchasing
Customer machining waste and internally generated manufacturing scrap. Primary-metal smelter suppliers and exact return-stream shares undisclosed. Company identifies purpose-built Manchester recycling capability. S51 S52
Grade and impurities
Return value and acceptance depend on alloy identity and contamination; no representative incoming-feed Mg content or numerical oil/water limits verified. S51
Consumption and boundaries
No disclosed scrap-to-recovered-alloy mass balance or primary top-up ratio.
Supply dependencies
Segregation and contaminated machining waste constrain recycling. At group level, some alloy rare-earth inputs are sourced from China; named magnesium-smelter suppliers remain unverified. S51 S68

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Accept segregated machining returns → prepare/remelt compatible metal → adjust/refine alloy → cast/work material and machine finished parts → return internal scrap to recycling. No virgin ore-to-metal extraction is established at the covered operation. S51 S52
