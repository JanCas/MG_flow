Company and facilities

Ownership
Private company; present beneficial ownership not established. S13
Main products
Magnesium metal; integrated ferrosilicon, semi-coke and coal tar

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Dolomite; ferrosilicon; small fluorite addition. Dolomite is first calcined, then ground and briquetted with the reductant.
Source and purchasing
Dolomite quarry/supplier undisclosed. Site has 10,000 t/y ferrosilicon and 300,000 t/y semi-coke capacities; these establish integration, not proof that all reductant is internally supplied. S13
Grade and impurities
No feed MgO assay or impurity limits provided. Reported grade-one/grade-two product proportions are product classification yields, not ore recovery.
Consumption and boundaries
No t dolomite/t Mg or reductant consumption disclosed.
Supply dependencies
Documented industrial integration links production to ferrosilicon and semi-coke operations; no specific supply interruption established.

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Calcine dolomite → grind with ferrosilicon and a small fluorite addition → briquette → Pidgeon vacuum reduction → refine crude magnesium and cast saleable ingot. This route is explicitly described for this facility. S13
