Rare Earth Magnesium Technology Group (REMT)

Xinjiang Tengxiang, Hami; Baishan Tianan, Jilin

Company and facilities

Ownership
HKEX 00601, historically within Century Sunshine group; operating subsidiaries must not be counted again as independent group output. S15
Main products
Magnesium metal/alloy products; Baishan also ore powder, excluded from metal output

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Dolomite for thermal production; ferrosilicon reductant. Hami energy integration relies on semi-coke gas.
Source and purchasing
Baishan circular describes a captive dolomite mine about 12 km from the plant, historical mining capacity 450,000 t/y and 23.027 million t resource as of 2009. These facts do not establish feed to Hami, whose quarry source is undisclosed. S17
Grade and impurities
No mine-specific MgO/Mg assay or prepared-charge impurity analysis verified.
Consumption and boundaries
Mine capacity is not feed consumption; no defensible t ore/t Mg ratio.
Supply dependencies
Hami environmental rectification requires semi-coke facilities to be upgraded; financing constraints prompted suspension arrangements. Current restart date unverified. S16

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Historical dolomite calcination → thermal reduction → refining/alloy production. During Hami upgrades the group plans trading and commissioned processing; purchased finished metal/alloy is therefore distinct from its own primary production. Baishan’s newly commercial ore-powder line is not a magnesium smelter. S15 S16

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
Own primary production undisclosed. Magnesium-product sales: 12,373 t in 2025 versus 13,678 t in 2024. S15 Newer interim filing text reports H1 2026 magnesium-product sales of 1,680 t versus 6,165 t in H1 2025; the indexed mirror was read but the official PDF was not retrieved. These partial-year sales do not replace annual production. S92
Capacity, t/y
Historical Hami 45,000 and Baishan 75,000 t/y entries are not treated as currently available primary capacity. S14
Interpretation and recent trend
Annual product sales fell about 9.5% in 2025 (calculated). The mirror-supported H1 comparison indicates a further decline, on a different reporting period. Trading/toll processing and inventory movements prevent treating sales as own primary production. S15 S92

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
Longstanding suppliers/customers acknowledged but unnamed; trading and toll processing intended to maintain service. S16
Intermediate products
Purchased or own magnesium → alloy/products; ore powder separate
Final applications
Company-specific named final applications not established.
Relationship basis
Current counterparties undisclosed

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
2026 own-production status, restart, inventory/trading split and mine allocation are unresolved.
