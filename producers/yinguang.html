Yinguang / Yinguang Huasheng

Wenxi, Shanxi; Yinguang Huasheng and related group facilities

Primary + alloys + fabricationCommercial group; recent downstream activity documented

Company and facilities

Ownership
Yinguang group; private corporate structure and present ultimate shareholdings not fully resolved. S08 S09
Main products
Primary magnesium, alloys, extrusions/forgings and wheel blanks, cast magnesium anodes

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Dolomite for primary metal; ferrosilicon reductant; primary metal then enters the alloy/fabrication chain.
Source and purchasing
Company describes integrated mineral-to-metal operations in Wenxi. Exact quarry, annual mine draw and external purchase share not disclosed in reviewed material. S08
Grade and impurities
Dolomite MgO content, moisture basis, silica/iron limits and prepared blend grade are undisclosed.
Consumption and boundaries
No documented feed-to-primary-metal ratio found.
Supply dependencies
No facility-specific current feed disruption established; mining and smelting integration does not eliminate reductant and energy dependencies.

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Dolomite → calcined feed → thermal reduction → crude metal refining/alloying → wrought/forged products. Integrated operations are supported, but a current Huasheng-specific reduction specification was not found; this route is a synthesis rather than an equipment-level verification. S08 S09

Read the detailed production-process guide ↗

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
31,000 t primary Mg at Huasheng, 2017; historical association figure. S05
Capacity, t/y
Huasheng: 65,000 t/y primary and 30,000 t/y alloy claimed. Group listing: 100,000 t/y primary; these overlap and are not additive. S08 S09
Interpretation and recent trend
Recent reporting of wheel-blank production supports downstream activity but does not establish present primary output or smelter utilization. S10

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
Newport Metals describes itself as the exclusive North American distributor of Yinguang cast magnesium anodes. Individual end buyers remain unnamed. Internal fabrication also consumes metal. S83 S08
Intermediate products
Magnesium alloy → cast sacrificial anodes; separate alloy → forged wheel blanks
Final applications
Yinguang cast anodes distributed into pipe and tank corrosion protection; separate company-supported vehicle wheel chain. Newport’s other extruded products are not all assigned to Yinguang. S83 S10
Relationship basis
Current distributor page (undated); internal fabrication; end customers unnamed

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
Group versus subsidiary boundary and current primary actuals are unresolved; marketing capacity is not annual output.
