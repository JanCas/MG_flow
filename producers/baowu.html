China / Producer profile

Baowu Magnesium (formerly Yunhai)

Chaohu, Anhui; Wutai, Shanxi; associate Anhui Baomei at Qingyang; Gansu Baomei supplies ferrosilicon

Company and facilities

Ownership
China Baowu-controlled through Baosteel Metal; Qingyang is an associate and must not be counted as wholly consolidated. S02
Main products
Primary ingot, magnesium alloys, diecast/extruded components; also substantial aluminum business

Feedstock and preparation

Starting material
Dolomite; ferrosilicon reductant and alloy additions. Raw carbonate rock is distinct from calcined dolime entering the reduction blend.
Source and purchasing
Controlled dolomite resources: Chaohu about 90 million t, Wutai 580 million t; associate Baomei at Qingyang about 1.3 billion t. H1 2026 report confirms Chaohu mining, while Wutai mine safety-design approval was still progressing. These are resource holdings, not annual feed. Gansu’s approximately 14 million t quartz resource supports the silicon/ferrosilicon chain, not the magnesium mineral input. The first furnace of Gansu Baomei’s planned 300,000 t/y FeSi project started in May 2026, prioritizing internal Mg plants. Purchased suppliers remain unnamed. S82
Grade and impurities
No facility-linked dolomite MgO/Mg assay, impurity analysis or prepared-charge specification located. Resource tonnage does not establish metallurgical grade.
Consumption and boundaries
No disclosed plant-boundary t dry dolomite/t primary Mg or reductant consumption found.
Supply dependencies
Wutai mine approvals and Gansu FeSi ramp-up constrain how resource ownership can be interpreted as available captive supply. The full FeSi project nameplate is not the capacity of the first commissioned furnace. Qingyang mining was partly developed in January 2026. S82 S04

Production process

Route at the covered facility
Mine and crush dolomite → calcine to dolime and prepare a ferrosilicon-bearing charge → vacuum silicothermic reduction using the group’s vertical-retort technology → condense/refine magnesium → sell ingot/alloy or consume it in component production. The filing describes the group route; a plant-by-plant technology allocation is not given. S02

Output and capacity

Actual output and reporting year
No recent magnesium-only annual production located in the reviewed 2025 and H1 2026 filings. S02 S82
Capacity, t/y
100,000 t/y primary and 200,000 t/y alloy baseline in ESG disclosure. Separate projects: Chaohu +50,000; Wutai 100,000; associate Baomei 300,000 t/y primary. These are not summed as operating capacity. S03
Interpretation and recent trend
2025 nonferrous production 469,101.56 t versus 460,970.44 t in 2024 includes aluminum and other products. It is excluded from the magnesium-output comparison. The group is a leading integrated producer, but its current primary-only rank cannot be verified. S02 H1 2026 reporting says Anhui Baomei crude-metal and alloy volumes rose substantially during ramp-up, but gives no absolute comparable magnesium tonnage. S82

Buyers and applications

Documented buyers / partners
Geely StarDrive, VREMT and ZF: disclosed batch component deliveries. Other vehicle-program nominations are future/qualification relationships, not confirmed metal purchases. S02 A May 2026 YIZUMI announcement documents magnesium component mass production for SERES and Baowu; this is downstream supply, not an ingot offtake volume. S90
Intermediate products
Internally made magnesium alloy → motor housings and other diecast components
Final applications
Company-specific vehicle drivetrain/lightweight components; separate overseas metal/alloy markets in Europe, North America and Southeast Asia. S02 S04
Relationship basis
Current component customers; not necessarily buyers of magnesium ingot

Evidence gaps

What remains unresolved
Primary output by facility, realized expansion utilization, feed assays/ratios, external ingot buyers and associate attribution remain unresolved.
