Purity is only one part of quality

A buyer purchases a defined material, not simply “magnesium.” Unalloyed ingot, a casting alloy, a powder and a sacrificial anode have different acceptance criteria. ASTM B92/B92M-17(2025) covers unalloyed ingot and stick for remelting; ISO 8287:2021 covers cast unalloyed magnesium made by thermal or electrolytic routes. Neither process name alone establishes the finished grade. S95 S96

Bulk assay tells how much magnesium is present. Individual impurity limits control what the remainder may contain. As a mass-fraction illustration, 99.9% Mg leaves at most 0.1%, or 1,000 ppm, for everything else combined; it does not allow 1,000 ppm of every impurity. A separate 0.001% limit corresponds to 10 ppm. These conversions explain the units, not a universal acceptance threshold.

A high-quality magnesium alloy intentionally contains other metals, so a lower Mg percentage need not mean lower quality. Nor does a high metal assay establish freedom from oxide inclusions, surface contamination or unsuitable particle size. Iron-related corrosion is documented experimentally; its effect depends on the material and environment, so no single corrosion-tolerance number is assigned to all products. S98 S95 S97 S99

Follow the metal—and distinguish the transaction

The physical route and the commercial relationship are separate. A producer may sell directly, use a distributor, or supply an integrated downstream business. Traders can arrange marketing, procurement, transport and working capital; a trader’s sales volume is therefore not additional primary production. Wogen describes these services, but the package used in each magnesium contract is not public. S102

A tolling arrangement pays a processor to convert material supplied by another party; the contract determines ownership and metal accounting. In a scrap buy-back arrangement, the recycler instead purchases the scrap and sells resulting metal. These are different commercial models, not assertions about the terms of every company profiled. REMT explicitly reports trading/tolling activities. S15

New scrap comes from manufacturing; old scrap comes from products after use. Recycling magnesium contained in aluminum alloys may keep that magnesium inside an aluminum product rather than yield merchant Mg ingot. That is why a recycled-magnesium statistic cannot automatically be added to magnesium-ingot availability. USGS explicitly includes recovery at secondary aluminum smelters. S01

Regional warehouses and alloy conversion can put an exporting country into trade statistics even when it has little or no primary smelting. Conversely, magnesium already incorporated in aluminum sheet or vehicle parts travels under those products’ classifications. Customs data therefore cannot trace every tonne back to its smelter or forward to its final application without additional records. This is an accounting inference, not a quantified re-export estimate.

How commercial prices are formed

Magnesium is bought through negotiated physical transactions and supply contracts. The 2023 USITC record describes spot, annual and longer-term sales; many detailed shares are confidential. Its historical contract mix is not imposed on today’s global market. A price assessment is a reference for a specified trade, not the realized price of every producer. S49

These benchmark examples show why purity, product form, location, delivery basis, shipment size, timing and payment terms belong alongside a price. They are definitions, not live quotations.

FOB (free on board) places delivery on board the vessel at the named shipment port. CIF (cost, insurance and freight) includes seller-arranged freight and insurance to the named destination port, but delivery risk transfers earlier, at shipment. DDP (delivered duty paid) puts more destination obligations on the seller. In-warehouse identifies a storage point; duty-paid status still needs to be stated. These terms allocate responsibilities, not a single universal landed price. S105 S100

To compare offers, first align date, currency, product and unit; then account for costs not already included: freight and insurance, duties and clearance, local delivery, financing, and conversion or qualification costs. The applicable origin, grade and contract can change the result. This is a comparison framework, not a quoted premium schedule. Do not add freight or duty twice to a delivered quotation.

For unit conversion, 1 US cent/lb is approximately USD 22.0462 per metric tonne. That arithmetic removes a unit difference only; it does not reconcile 99.8% ingot with 93%-basis alloy or remove regional trade barriers. A customs value divided by shipment mass is an average unit value, not automatically a like-for-like spot price.

Magnesium compounds belong to separate material markets and classifications. A magnesium-oxide price or shipment is not a magnesium-metal quotation or tonne. For a defensible flow dataset, retain reporter, partner, year, product code, net mass, value, origin/re-export basis and any conversion to Mg content. Apparent consumption requires domestic production plus imports minus exports on a consistent basis, with inventory changes accounted for. Tracing material into final products also requires transformation losses; net imports alone are not consumption. S54

This review does not establish a complete 2025 or 2026 bilateral global trade matrix, producer-to-buyer tonnages, private impurity ceilings or contractual premiums. It provides documented routes, specification examples and clearly bounded regional statistics. Project offtake, such as Magrathea’s future Wogen agreement, remains separate from operating trade flows. S59
