# Accuracy, freshness and readability review

Review completed: **27 September 2026**. The research cutoff is unchanged.

Two agents reviewed the work independently: one checked readability and reporting clarity across all profiles and the generator; the other checked accuracy and newer evidence for the established primary producers. The lead researcher checked recycler/alloy businesses and all five projects, verified material findings, and applied the corrections. This was a targeted public-source review, not an independent plant audit or a guarantee that every available document was found.

At completion of the independent review, the source register included **94 references**, up from 66. The 28 additions include both newer disclosures and older documents that resolve evidence gaps. A newer publication does not necessarily contain newer production data.

## Material corrections

- Huiye’s liquidation and Ferdows’ reported idling are now visible in the directory and profiles.
- Qinghai’s documented trial metal is included without converting campaign output into an annual figure or treating liquid metal and ingots as separate production. The conflicting scopes of later reporting remain explicit.
- Baowu’s resource ownership is separated from operating mine supply and ferrosilicon commissioning. Yinguang’s Newport distribution channel is now documented.
- Magrathea’s JV formation is confirmed. Tergeo’s former site is covered as redevelopment. MPI’s first phases make compounds; a metal phase is conditional.
- Magontec, Luxfer, REMT, Regal, Verde and Latrobe have more current or more precise evidence, with sales, alloy output, pilot scales and capacities kept on their original bases.

Detailed evidence and locators are in the [source catalog](sources.html) and [downloadable register](data/sources.csv). The updated [report](report.md), [website](index.html), CSV exports and ZIP bundle use the same corrected records.
